--- layout: default title: "UniRaid Rally: VW Golf II GTD Preparation & Management" parent: Projects nav_order: 2 --- # UniRaid Rally: VW Golf II GTD Preparation & Field Engineering **Operational Management, Budget Control, and Real-World Problem Solving** ### 1. Project Scope & Realities Driving a 1987 Volkswagen Golf II GTD across 3,000 km of Moroccan desert with 100 kg of cargo is a major logistical challenge. With limited resources, zero telemetric sensors, and harsh field conditions, the success of the rally relied entirely on preparation quality, cost control, and rapid manual diagnostics. My teammate and I managed the project from initial budgeting and sponsor acquisition through to on-event driving, navigation, and repair. ### 2. Sourcing, Logistics & Weight Management * **Budget & Sponsorship**: Managed the commercial side of the project, securing **over €6,000** in funding and building partnerships with **15+ technical sponsors**. * **Workshop Coordination**: Planned and coordinated the mechanic work done by our partners, including suspension checks, fluid overhauls, brake maintenance, gearbox servicing, and steering alignment. * **Payload Logistics**: Balanced the vehicle's handling by planning the layout and securing the 100 kg load of solidarity cargo and tools. #### A. Resource Planning and Spare Parts Allocation ![Budget & Logistics Excel Sheet](../assets/images/budget_logistics_excel.png) *This sheet tracks our spending and mechanical priority list. It shows how we prioritized spare parts (belts, filters, and tire-repair tools) while staying strictly under our financial cap and physical weight limits.* ### 3. Preparation & Preventative Steps Before leaving, we focused heavily on preventative maintenance and physical packaging to handle desert roads: * **Cargo Security**: Sourced and installed a reliable roof bar setup to distribute weight evenly and safely. * **Basic Mechanical Prep**: Replaced fuel and air filters, inspected all critical hose clamps, tightened major structural bolts, and ensured baseline system integrity. #### B. Validation in the Field ![Volkswagen Golf II GTD on Track](../assets/images/uniraid-car.png) *This shows our Golf II GTD running under full load during a desert stage. Daily structural checks on the subframe, tire tread wear, and fluid levels were completed at every single bivouac stop.* ### 4. Field Troubleshooting & Empirical Diagnostics Without telemetry or complex diagnostic tools, identifying issues meant observing the car's behavior closely (temperatures, smells, sounds, and steering feedback): * **Cooling System Repairs**: Diagnosed and fixed pressurized coolant leaks in the middle of stages to avoid overheating. * **Filter Management**: Handled rapid fuel filter changes in the field when desert sand began restricting engine flow. * **Result**: Completed the entire 3,000 km rally with zero mechanical retirements, proving the value of simple, structured planning and solid prep.
