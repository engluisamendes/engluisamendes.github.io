Engineering Case Study 1: Automotive Paint Booth Environmental Control & Quasi-Digital Twin

MSc Thesis Project in Partnership with Toyota Caetano Portugal | Grade: 19/20

S - Situation

In automotive OEM paint facilities, strict environmental stability (temperature and relative humidity) within the paint spray booth is critical to prevent surface finishing defects and maintain compliance with Ryohin Joken ("100% Good Product Conditions") standards. Environmental fluctuations induced unquantified process drift during high-throughput continuous cycles.

T - Task

Design and validate an advanced environmental control scheme and a hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) Quasi-Digital Twin framework to maintain real-time atmospheric stability, conduct Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA), and minimize energy consumption.

A - Action

  • Sensor Architecture & FMEA: Executed a full sensor network layout and preliminary FMEA to mitigate thermal and moisture signal latency across critical booth zones.
  • Multi-Domain Physical Modeling: Developed dynamic thermal-fluid models of the air handling unit (AHU) inside MATLAB Simscape and Simulink.
  • Closed-Loop Control & Hardware Bridge: Integrated closed-loop PID and feedforward control logic operating on a physical Siemens S7-1200 PLC, linked via OPC UA protocol to the simulation environment for real-time telemetry validation.

R - Results

  • Reduced thermal variability within the paint booth chamber to within ±0.5°C and relative humidity tolerance to ±2% under variable intake conditions.
  • Eliminated paint cure non-conformances attributable to environmental drift, securing full Ryohin Joken process compliance.
  • Awarded an academic evaluation grade of 19/20 by the Master's Examination Board.

Engineering Case Study 2: Injection Mold Design & Validation for Automotive ABS Component

Individual mechanical tooling project executed in Siemens NX, focusing on complete mold design for an automotive ABS speaker housing component with complex undercut geometries.

Key Engineering Contributions

  • Kinematic Subsystems: Designed side-action sliders and mechanical angle lifters to enable collision-free release of internal clip undercuts.
  • Feed & Thermal Management: Configured hot-runner feeding system and conformal cooling channels to ensure uniform mold thermal gradient, reducing dynamic shrinkage and warpage risks.
  • Ejection & Tooling Calculations: Executed clamping force, core/cavity mechanical stress, and ejector pin load distribution calculations based on polymer shrinkage coefficients.

Engineering Case Study 3: Brass Drawing Process FEA & Taguchi Optimization

Nonlinear finite element analysis (FEA) using Abaqus/Explicit coupled with Taguchi Design of Experiments (DOE) to optimize the multi-pass deep drawing process of a brass alloy component.

Analytical & Numerical Scope

  • Elastoplastic Material Modeling: Implemented Johnson-Cook constitutive models to capture strain hardening, strain rate sensitivity, and thermal softening during heavy plastic deformation.
  • Defect Mitigation: Analyzed draw force dynamics, maximum tensile stress concentrations, die corner radius contact stresses, and elastic springback behavior.
  • Optimization Yield: Applied Taguchi orthogonal arrays to identify optimal friction coefficient, punch velocity, and blank holder pressure, reducing springback by 18% and peak draw force by 12%.
