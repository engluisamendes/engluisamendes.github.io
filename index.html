Master's Thesis · Toyota Caetano Portugal · 2026

Automotive Paint Booth Microclimate Control

Development and physical validation of an Industry 4.0 retrofit framework to stabilize microclimatic conditions in the Land Cruiser 70 top-coat line without replacing primary HVAC infrastructure.

PROJECT IMPACT
€2.7k/yr
Validated operational payback through closed-loop microclimate control and ATEX-compliant stabilization.
SITUATION
Top-Coat Microclimate Sensitivity

The top-coat application line required tighter relative humidity and thermal tolerances to maintain surface finish consistency across seasonal external climate shifts.

TASK
Brownfield Retrofit Strategy

Engineer a cost-effective, automated closed-loop control architecture to stabilize booth environmental parameters within existing HVAC infrastructure constraints.

ENGINEERING LIFECYCLE & EXECUTION WORKFLOW
1. Plant Gemba Diagnosis → 2. SPC & Statistics → 3. Project Management → 4. Technical Specs & Simscape → 5. Financial Analysis → 6. Supplier Procurement
Phase 01
Gemba Field Diagnostics & Problem Framing

Mapped operational shop-floor conditions in the Land Cruiser 70 top-coat booth, identifying seasonal climate instability as the primary cause of thermal-humidity drift.

Phase 02
SPC & Statistical Modeling (Minitab)

Constructed Laney U' control charts to handle defect overdispersion and used Pearson correlation analysis to establish the relationship between temperature drift and finish quality (r=0.788).

Phase 03
Project Management & Critical Path Scheduling

Structured work breakdown schedules, milestone Gantt charts, and critical path analysis to ensure retrofit implementation fits within planned plant maintenance windows.

Phase 04
Technical Specifications & Simscape Dynamic Validation

Specified ATEX-certified instrumentation, VFD-driven water pumps, and coordinated PLC logic. Built a lumped-parameter psychrometric model in MATLAB/Simscape to validate closed-loop stability across seasonal loads.

Phase 05 & 06
Financial Payback & Supplier Procurement

Conducted CAPEX/OPEX viability analysis yielding a net payback of €2,726/yr. Managed vendor requests for quotations (RFQs) and presented the complete deployment roadmap to Toyota Motor Europe stakeholders.

Tooling Engineering · Simoldes Group Collaboration

Automotive Speaker Injection Mold Design

Design of a high-precision polymer injection mold in Siemens NX for a complex automotive Polypropylene (PP) speaker grille in collaboration with Tier-1 supplier Simoldes Plastics.

TOOL MECHANICS
5 Lifters (8°)
Configured angled lifters and cam sliders to release complex internal undercuts with zero mechanical collision.
SITUATION
Complex Undercuts in Automotive Interior Part

The automotive speaker component required intricate aesthetic features and internal snap fits, presenting severe undercut ejection challenges in Polypropylene (PP).

TASK
Collision-Free Tool Architecture (DfM)

Design a robust, standardized injection mold ensuring zero mechanical interference, optimal thermal cooling, and standardized tooling base integration.

ENGINEERING LIFECYCLE & EXECUTION WORKFLOW
1. Part Feasibility → 2. OEM Client Specs → 3. 3D Tool Design (NX) → 4. Mold Filling Simulation
Phase 01
Part Geometry & Feasibility Analysis

Evaluated aesthetic surface constraints, wall thickness transitions, draft angles, and complex interior undercuts in Polypropylene (PP).

Phase 02
Client Requirements & Mold Base Selection

Integrated OEM supplier specifications into a standardized DME 2A tool base configuration with modular guiding and ejection mechanisms.

Phase 03
Complete 3D Tooling Design (Siemens NX)

Modeled core/cavity parting lines, 5 angled lifters set at an 8° inclination, dual cam sliders with plungers, and dedicated 8 mm cooling channels with internal baffles to control part warpage.

Phase 04
Injection & Filling Simulation (Temperature & Pressure)

Simulated melt front advancement, injection pressure boundaries, thermal cooling uniformity, and clamping forces to ensure complete filling without weld line defects or shrinkage sink marks.
