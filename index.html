Toyota Thesis Project

Automotive Paint Booth Environmental Control

MSc Thesis Grade: 19/20

Problem Statement

Uncontrolled ambient temperature and relative humidity shifts in paint spray booths cause subtle paint cure defects, violating Toyota's Ryohin Joken standards.

Technical Methodology

  • Engineered a Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Quasi-Digital Twin establishing communication between physical Siemens S7-1200 PLCs and MATLAB Simscape models via OPC UA.
  • Executed sensor network placement optimization and FMEA protocols to reduce reading latency.

Quantified Outcomes

  • Temperature stabilized to ±0.5°C and relative humidity to ±2%.
  • Model current draw correlated with physical hardware telemetry at r > 0.96.
