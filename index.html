Master's Thesis · Toyota Caetano Portugal · 2026

Automotive Paint Booth Microclimate Control (STAR)

Development and physical validation of an Industry 4.0 retrofit framework to stabilize microclimatic conditions in the Land Cruiser 70 top-coat line without replacing primary HVAC infrastructure.

PROJECT IMPACT
€2.7k/yr
Validated operational payback through closed-loop microclimate control and ATEX-compliant stabilization.
SITUATION
Top-Coat Microclimate Sensitivity

The top-coat application line required tighter relative humidity and thermal tolerances to maintain surface finish consistency across seasonal external climate shifts.

TASK
Brownfield Retrofit Strategy

Engineer a cost-effective, automated closed-loop control architecture to stabilize booth environmental parameters within existing HVAC infrastructure constraints.

ACTION & RESULTS (STAR)
ACTION 01
Root Cause Diagnostics & Statistical Modeling (TPS / SPC)
Conducted on-site Gemba mapping and diagnostic FMEA. Used Minitab to build Laney U' control charts that handled defect overdispersion, while Pearson correlation proved direct links between temperature and paint sagging (r=0.788).
ACTION 02
Hardware Architecture & Simscape Dynamic Validation
Specified ATEX-certified instrumentation, VFD-driven pumps, and coordinated PLC logic. Built a lumped-parameter psychrometric model in MATLAB/Simscape to validate multi-variable control boundaries under seasonal extreme loads prior to deployment.
Toyota Paint Booth Automation System Architecture & PLC Control Topology
RESULT
Validated Process Repeatability & Risk Mitigation
Simscape simulations verified that coordinated control eliminates transient humidity drops from sensible heating. Delivered a fully scoped technical and investment roadmap directly to Toyota Motor Europe stakeholders.
Tooling Engineering · Simoldes Group Collaboration

Automotive Speaker Injection Mold Design (STAR)

Design of a high-precision polymer injection mold in Siemens NX for a complex automotive Polypropylene (PP) speaker grille in collaboration with Tier-1 supplier Simoldes Plastics.

TOOL MECHANICS
5 Lifters (8°)
Configured angled lifters and cam sliders to release complex internal undercuts with zero mechanical collision.
SITUATION
Complex Undercuts in Automotive Interior Part

The automotive speaker component required intricate aesthetic features and internal snap fits, presenting severe undercut ejection challenges in Polypropylene (PP).

TASK
Collision-Free Tool Architecture (DfM)

Design a robust, standardized injection mold ensuring zero mechanical interference, optimal thermal cooling, and standardized tooling base integration.

ACTION & RESULTS (STAR)
ACTION 01
Advanced Surface Modeling & Undercut Kinematics
Executed Patch Surfaces, Isocline parting line checks, and Trim Region operations in Siemens NX. Engineered 5 angled lifters set at an 8° inclination and single/dual cam-pin sliders with plungers for side geometries.
ACTION 02
Thermal Management & Tool Standardization
Configured standardized DME 2A tool base with straight ejector sleeves. Designed 8 mm cooling channels with internal baffles in high-heat core zones to mitigate Polypropylene shrinkage warpage and shorten cycle time.
Siemens NX Mold Assembly (DME 2A Base & Internal Kinematic Subsystems)
RESULT
Validated Tool Assembly & DfM Compliance
Delivered a fully validated, production-ready 3D CAD tool assembly adhering to tier-1 automotive manufacturing tolerances with fully detailed 2D manufacturing drawings.
