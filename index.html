Toyota Thesis Project

Automotive Paint Booth Environmental Control

MSc Thesis Evaluation: 19/20

Problem Statement

Uncontrolled ambient temperature and humidity shifts in paint spray booths cause subtle finish defects, violating Toyota's Ryohin Joken ("100% Good Product Conditions") standards.

Technical Methodology

  • Engineered a Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Quasi-Digital Twin linking physical Siemens S7-1200 PLCs with MATLAB Simscape thermal-fluid plant models via OPC UA protocol.
  • Executed sensor placement optimization and FMEA protocols to minimize signal reading latency.

Quantified Outcomes

  • Chamber temperature stabilized to within ±0.5°C and relative humidity to ±2%.
  • Model current draw correlated with physical hardware telemetry at r > 0.96.
Tooling Engineering

Injection Mold Design & Kinematic Validation

Engineering Scope

Industrial injection mold developed in Siemens NX for an automotive ABS speaker housing with complex undercut geometries.

Subsystems Integrated

  • Kinematics: Designed side-action sliders and mechanical angle lifters for collision-free undercut ejection.
  • Thermal Control: Configured conformal cooling loops to eliminate part warpage and optimize cycle time.
FEA Simulation

Brass Drawing Process FEA Optimisation

Numerical Analysis

Nonlinear finite element analysis using Abaqus/Explicit combined with Taguchi DOE to model multi-pass deep drawing of brass alloys.

Results

  • Post-forming elastic springback reduced by 18%.
  • Peak punch draw force reduced by 12%.
