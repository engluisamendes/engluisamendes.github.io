Automatic Climate Control For Painting Booth

Development of an Industry 4.0 climate control retrofit for Land Cruiser 70 assembly line (14 units/day)—from root-cause defect diagnostics to physical Simscape model validation.

NET PAYBACK
€2,726/yr
Direct CAPEX/OPEX net savings presented directly to Toyota Motor Europe (TME) stakeholders.
01
Statistical Defect Diagnostics & SPC

Evaluated a baseline defect rate of 5.96 defects/unit (100% repair rate) using Laney U-Charts, FMEA, Gemba, Genchi Genbutsu, and 5 Whys. Proved statistical correlation between paint sagging and temperature fluctuation via Pearson analysis.

02
Automation & Explosion-Proof ATEX Safety

Architected an automated PLC/HMI control strategy (Siemens ET200 + VFD Pump) integrating a strict 55% RH safety threshold to ensure ATEX safety compliance inside spray booth environments.

TECHNICAL EXECUTION & SIMULATION
01
Physical Dynamic Modeling (Simscape)
Constructed a physical dynamic simulation model in MATLAB Simscape to predict thermal and humidity transients across dual Air Supply Units (ASU), delivering actionable technical specifications and predictive maintenance roadmaps.

Tooling Engineering · Simoldes Group Collaboration

Polypropylene Injection Mold Tool Design

Engineering design of a complete polymer injection mold in Siemens NX for an automotive Polypropylene (PP) speaker component, ensuring zero mechanical interference during dynamic ejection.

TOOL MECHANICS
5 Lifters
Configured 5 angled lifters set at an 8° inclination to successfully release interior undercut features.
01
Surface & Subsystem Modeling

Executed Patch Surfaces, Isocline checks, Check Regions, and Trim Region modeling in Siemens NX. Integrated single and dual cam-pin sliders with plungers for side-action geometries.

02
Standardized Tool Base & Thermal Control

Standardized a DME 2A tool base with straight ejector sleeves. Designed 8 mm cooling channels with internal baffles in high-heat core zones to optimize cycle time and manage Polypropylene rheology/shrinkage.

TECHNICAL EXECUTION & WORKAROUNDS
01
Rheology Analysis & Solver Optimization
Evaluated polymer filling behavior and gate locations to eliminate short shots and weld lines. Overcame Siemens NX memory violation solver crashes by prioritizing theoretical 8 mm runner sizing and 2D tool detailing.
