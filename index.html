In-Depth Case Studies & Technical Breakthroughs

Toyota Caetano Portugal | Master's Thesis

Automotive Paint Booth Environmental Control & Quasi-Digital Twin

During high-throughput vehicle production, ambient temperature and humidity shifts inside paint spray booths cause subtle paint cure defects. To align with Ryohin Joken ("100% Good Product Conditions") standards, I architected a Quasi-Digital Twin.

Key Accomplishments & Methodology:
  • Hardware-in-the-Loop Integration: Linked physical Siemens S7-1200 PLCs and Sinamics VFDs to a dynamic MATLAB Simscape model via OPC UA protocol.
  • FMEA & Quality Control: Performed Failure Mode and Effects Analysis on sensor response latency, stabilizing booth temperature to within ±0.5°C and relative humidity to ±2%.
  • Academic Outcome: Defended before the examination board and awarded a grade of 19/20.
CAD & Tooling Engineering

Siemens NX Injection Mold Design for ABS Component

Engineered an industrial injection mold for a complex automotive ABS speaker grille featuring intricate interior locking clips and tight aesthetic tolerances.

Key Accomplishments & Methodology:
  • Kinematic Subsystems: Designed side-action mechanical sliders and angle lifters to resolve deep internal undercuts without mechanical collision.
  • Thermal & Ejection Analysis: Configured balanced hot-runner feed gates, cooling lines, and ejection pin layouts calculated against material shrinkage rates to eliminate warpage.
FEA & Materials Optimization

Nonlinear FEA Deep Drawing Simulation (Abaqus/Explicit)

Analyzed elastoplastic behavior during multi-pass deep drawing of brass alloys using non-linear finite element modeling and statistical DOE techniques.

Key Accomplishments & Methodology:
  • Stress Analysis: Mapped Von Mises stress concentrations, blank-holder contact pressure, and die radius shear behavior under high deformation rates.
  • Taguchi Optimization: Reduced elastic springback by 18% and peak draw force by 12% by optimizing friction parameters and blank holder force.
Field Engineering & Automotive Mechanics

UniRaid Desert Rally Vehicle Preparation & Reliability

Prepared and field-serviced a 1987 Volkswagen Golf II GTD for a 3,000 km desert endurance rally carrying 100 kg of humanitarian aid across harsh terrain.

Key Accomplishments & Methodology:
  • Thermal Management: Designed auxiliary high-flow cooling circuits and fan overrides to prevent engine thermal runaway in 40°C+ desert temperatures.
  • Field Diagnostics: Executed emergency structural chassis welding, suspension tuning, and mechanical root-cause analysis under extreme resource constraints.
