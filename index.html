Toyota Caetano Portugal | MSc Thesis

Automotive Paint Booth Environmental Control

MSc Thesis Evaluation: 19/20

Industrial Partner & Statistical Quality Focus

Developed directly within Toyota Caetano Portugal's OEM manufacturing plant. Integrated Statistical Process Control (SPC) in Minitab with real-time Simscape physical modeling to uphold Ryohin Joken ("100% Good Product Conditions").

Automation Schematic Viewer

Open Vector PDF Schematic in Full Window →

Technical Highlights

  • Simscape Multi-Domain Modeling: Modeled thermodynamic heat exchange, moisture distribution, and fan torque profiles.
  • Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL): Linked physical Siemens S7-1200 PLCs and Sinamics VFDs to Simscape via OPC UA.
  • Minitab SPC Validation: Analyzed process capability ($C_p, C_{pk}$) and performed Anderson-Darling tests on continuous telemetry data.

Quantified Outcomes

  • Chamber temperature stabilized to within ±0.5°C and relative humidity to ±2%.
  • Pearson correlation between hardware current draw and simulation model reached r > 0.96.
